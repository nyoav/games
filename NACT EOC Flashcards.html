NACT Fundamentals and Selection 1 / 30

What is the clinical definition and primary goal of neoadjuvant chemotherapy (NACT) in epithelial ovarian cancer (EOC)?

Answer

Systemic chemotherapy administered before surgical debulking to reduce tumor size and make a subsequent "interval" surgery safer and more effective.
